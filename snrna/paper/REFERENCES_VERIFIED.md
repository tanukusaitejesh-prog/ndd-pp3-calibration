## B. UNVERIFIED items and corrections to your descriptions

None of the 57 items is UNVERIFIED: each one resolves to a real record. The following descriptions were wrong, imprecise, or incomplete:

- **B-3 (Greene 2025):** Confirmed as Nat Genet 57:1367-1373. The variants are dominant/de novo RNU2-2 variants, and the phenotype is an NDD "with prominent epilepsy".
- **B-4 (Jackson 2025):** The paper is not only about RNU2-2. It identifies RNU2-2 and RNU5B-1 as NDD genes through an analysis of R-loop forming regions. A Publisher Correction exists (57:1792).
- **B-5 (Nava 2025):** The genes are RNU4-2 (U4), RNU5B-1 (U5; established as an NDD gene) and RNU5A-1 (U5; proposed as a strong candidate only). Your volume, pages and DOI are correct.
- **B-7 (Greene 2026):** The recessive gene is RNU2-2, not RNU4-2. A Publisher Correction exists (58:1187).
- **B-8, B-11, B-42, B-47, B-53, B-54, B-57:** All are still preprints, with no peer-reviewed version found as of 4 Nov 2026.
- **B-11 (Messaoud):** "snRNA constraint scan" undersells this paper. It covers more than 300 spliceosome protein-coding genes and 43 snRNA genes, plus snRNA pseudogenes, in a rare-disease cohort. It uses population variant-depletion (constrained-region) analysis and random-forest prioritisation of pseudogenes. The exact title is "Analysis of spliceosome-related coding and noncoding genes and pseudogenes reveals novel candidates".
- **B-12 (Leitão):** Found: Nat Genet 2026;58(4):782-797, doi:10.1038/s41588-026-02547-5. It covers both dominant and recessive RNU2-2 variants in DEE, not only one inheritance mode.
- **B-13 (Quinodoz):** The DOI has a 2025 prefix (s41588-025-...), but the article is in the January 2026 issue (58:169-179). The U6 genes are RNU6-1, RNU6-2, RNU6-8 and RNU6-9.
- **B-17 (Xing 2021):** The journal is Human Mutation (42:1042-1052), doi:10.1002/humu.24239.
- **B-18 (Johnson 2026):**
  - The first author's initials are "MB", not "M".
  - The paper covers RNU6ATAC and RNU4ATAC, and the phenotype is syndromic monogenic autoimmune diabetes, not an NDD.
